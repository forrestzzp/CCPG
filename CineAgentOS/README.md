## AI 原生影视制片操作系统

**从分散的生成工具，走向连续、可协同、可审阅的影视生产。**

CineAgentOS 正在构建面向电影、剧集、短剧、广告、动画与虚拟制作团队的 AI 原生影视生产操作系统。它希望把项目上下文、创作资产、多模型能力、团队协作、审核决策与交付流程连接起来，让 AI 从单点生成工具进入真实制作流程。

我们相信，下一阶段的影视 AI 竞争，不只是谁能生成一段更漂亮的视频，而是谁能帮助团队把创意稳定地推进为可协作、可复核、可持续迭代的制作成果。

## 行业机会

生成式 AI 正在快速扩大视听内容的创作空间，但制作团队仍要面对几个实际问题：

- 脚本、角色设定、场景参考、镜头素材与生成结果分散在不同工具中，项目上下文容易断裂。
- 单次生成不等于可交付内容；角色、场景、风格和镜头之间需要持续管理与人工把关。
- 模型和服务不断变化，团队需要灵活调用不同能力，同时保留自己的制作流程与资产组织方式。
- 真实制片需要多人协作、版本判断、审核和交付，不能只依靠个人提示词与临时文件夹。

CineAgentOS 聚焦的正是“生成能力”与“制作系统”之间的连接层。

## 产品方向

CineAgentOS 的产品设计围绕一条连续的制作工作流展开：

1. **项目与创作上下文**：围绕项目组织创意、剧本、角色、场景、镜头和制作素材。
2. **多模型制作能力**：根据任务连接文本、图像、视频、声音、视觉理解及后期制作工具；保持对不同模型与服务的适配空间。
3. **资产连续性与迭代**：让参考素材、生成版本、镜头结果和制作决策能够在项目语境中被查找、比较和继续处理。
4. **协作、审核与交付**：支持团队在创作过程中进行评审、修改、确认和交接，让 AI 参与生产而不替代有权人员作出最终决定。

这些内容描述的是产品方向。具体功能、服务接入和部署方式以经过核验的版本说明与演示为准。

## CineAgentOS 的产品判断

- **工作流优先**：不止提供单次内容生成，而是面向从创意开发到素材审核与交付的连续制作过程。
- **多模型适配**：不把产品绑定到单一模型；让制作团队能够按任务、质量、成本和可用性选择适合的工具。
- **面向团队生产**：把项目上下文、创作资产与审核决策纳入共同工作空间，减少工具切换造成的信息断裂。
- **创作者保持主导**：AI 提供创作与制作辅助，关键创意判断和交付确认由团队掌握。

## English

### AI-Native Film & Media Production Operating System

**From disconnected generation tools to continuous, collaborative, reviewable production.**

CineAgentOS is building an AI-native production operating system for film, television, short-form drama, advertising, animation, and virtual production teams. The product aims to connect project context, creative assets, multi-model capabilities, team collaboration, review decisions, and delivery workflows—bringing AI from isolated generation into the production process.

We believe the next phase of AI in media will be shaped not only by who can generate a compelling clip, but by who can help teams turn creative intent into work that can be coordinated, reviewed, and iterated throughout production.

### The opportunity

Generative AI is expanding what audiovisual teams can create. Production still involves practical gaps:

- Scripts, character and location references, shot materials, and generated outputs are spread across tools, fragmenting project context.
- A single generation is not a production-ready deliverable; continuity, review, and human decisions remain essential.
- Models and services change quickly, so teams need flexibility without losing their own workflows and asset organization.
- Professional production depends on collaboration, version decisions, review, and delivery—not only individual prompts and temporary folders.

CineAgentOS focuses on the layer connecting generative capabilities with production systems.

### Product direction

CineAgentOS is designed around a continuous production workflow:

1. **Project context:** Organize creative intent, scripts, characters, locations, shots, and production assets around a project.
2. **Multi-model production capabilities:** Connect text, image, video, audio, visual understanding, and post-production tools as appropriate to each task.
3. **Asset continuity and iteration:** Keep references, generated versions, shot outcomes, and production decisions discoverable and actionable in project context.
4. **Collaboration, review, and delivery:** Support review, revision, approval, and handoff while keeping final creative and delivery decisions with authorized people.

These points describe product direction. Specific features, integrations, and deployment options are subject to verification against a dated release and demonstration.

### Product thesis

- **Workflow-first:** Built for the production journey from creative development through asset review and delivery, rather than one-off generation.
- **Multi-model by design:** Avoids dependence on a single model and gives teams room to select tools based on task, quality, cost, and availability.
- **For production teams:** Connects project context, creative assets, and review decisions in a shared workspace.
- **Creator-led:** AI assists creative and production work; people retain control of consequential creative and delivery decisions.

### Investment thesis

As generative capabilities are offered through an evolving range of models and services, professional teams still need production workflows built around project context, asset continuity, collaboration, review, and delivery. CineAgentOS aims to become the collaboration layer connecting those capabilities with professional audiovisual production.

The long-term commercial case must be validated with production teams, including workflow usage, collaboration efficiency, integration cost, continued use, and willingness to pay. Investment and industry partnerships can focus on productization, model and tool ecosystem integrations, and studio pilots.

### Who we want to work with

CineAgentOS is intended for film and media production companies, production teams, advertising and animation studios, virtual production teams, and model or tool providers seeking to serve professional content workflows. We welcome partners who want to validate the product against real production needs.
